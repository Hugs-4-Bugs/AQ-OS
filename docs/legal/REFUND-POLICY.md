## 1. Overview

1.1. At AcquisitionOS, we want you to be satisfied with the Service. This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds are available for our subscription plans, how to request a refund, and what to expect during the process. By subscribing, you agree to this policy, which forms part of our [Terms of Service](TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md).

1.2. Current plan pricing for reference:

| Plan | Monthly | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0 / $0 (no charge) | — |
| Pro | ₹2,499/month ($29) | ₹23,990/year ($279) |
| Elite | ₹7,999/month ($89) | ₹76,790/year ($849) |

1.3. All amounts are exclusive of applicable taxes (GST is added for Indian customers at the prevailing rate; see Section 9).

## 2. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

2.1. We offer a **14-day money-back guarantee** on first-time subscription purchases. If you are not satisfied with the Service for any reason, you may request a **full refund** within **14 calendar days** of your initial subscription purchase.

2.2. **To be eligible for the money-back guarantee:**

- The request must be made within **14 calendar days** of the initial purchase date.
- This applies to the **first subscription purchase only** (i.e., first-time Pro or Elite purchases) — it does not apply to renewals, re-subscriptions after a prior cancellation, or plan upgrades from a previously refunded subscription.
- Your account must not have been terminated or suspended for violations of our [Terms of Service](TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md) or [Acceptable Use Policy](ACCEPTABLE-USE-POLICY.md).
- The refund applies to the subscription fee only; add-on credit purchases are handled separately (Section 5).
- Purchases made with promotional or coupon codes that were explicitly marked "non-refundable" are excluded.

2.3. The money-back guarantee applies regardless of whether you used the Service during the 14-day period. Consuming AI credits or sending outreach during the trial of the paid features does not disqualify you from the guarantee.

2.4. Upon an approved money-back refund, your subscription reverts to the Free plan (with the Free plan's 50 leads/month quota and 50 requests/hour API rate limit) or is downgraded as applicable.

2.5. **Worked examples.**

| Scenario | Outcome |
|---|---|
| You buy Pro monthly on Jan 5 and request a refund on Jan 12 | Within 14 days of first purchase → **full refund** of the ₹2,499 (+GST collected), account reverts to Free |
| You buy Pro monthly on Jan 5 and request a refund on Jan 25 | Outside the 14-day window → **no refund**; you may cancel, keeping access until the period ends |
| You buy Elite annual on Mar 1 (first purchase) and request a refund on Mar 10 | Within 14 days → **full refund** of ₹76,790 (+GST) |
| You renew Pro monthly for a second month and request a refund 3 days in | Renewal → **not covered** by the guarantee; no refund |
| You buy Pro, get a refund under the guarantee in January, and subscribe again in April | Second purchase is a re-subscription → **guarantee does not apply again** |

## 3. Prorated Refunds for Annual Plans

3.1. Outside the 14-day money-back guarantee, annual subscriptions are refundable only in **limited cases**, at our discretion and in accordance with this section:

- **Chronic service failure:** if we fail to meet the uptime commitment in the [SLA](SLA.md) for 3 consecutive months, you may terminate your annual subscription and receive a **prorated refund for the unused full months** remaining in your billing period.
- **Downgrade of an annual plan at our initiative** (for example, if we discontinue a plan you purchased), in which case we will refund the unused full months of the price difference.
- **Duplicate or erroneous charges:** charged twice for the same period, or charged after cancellation — fully refunded for the incorrect charge.

3.2. **Calculation.** Prorated refunds are calculated per **full unused month** remaining in the annual term. Partially used months are not refunded. Example: a Pro annual plan at ₹23,990 covers 12 months; if you cancel with 9 full months remaining, the refund is 9 × ₹1,999 (the effective monthly equivalent of the annual price) minus any applicable processing considerations described in this policy.

3.3. **Minimum threshold.** In line with our general billing terms, a prorated refund is issued only where the refund amount exceeds **₹100** (or the equivalent in your billing currency). Amounts at or below this threshold are issued as account credits instead.

3.4. **Monthly plans.** Monthly subscriptions are not refundable mid-period outside the 14-day money-back guarantee. When you cancel, you retain access until the end of the current monthly billing period (Section 6).

## 4. Non-Refundable Items

The following are **not** eligible for refunds:

- **Monthly plan payments after the 14-day money-back guarantee window** has expired (for first-time purchases) — including cancellations made mid-period; you retain access until the period ends.
- **Renewals** of any plan — the money-back guarantee applies to the initial purchase only.
- **Re-subscriptions** after a previous money-back refund or previous cancellation of the same plan.
- **Consumed AI credits** — credit add-on purchases where any credits have been consumed (partially or fully).
- **Expired credits** and **promotional/free credits** (these have no monetary value).
- **Accounts terminated for violations** of the [Terms of Service](TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md) or [Acceptable Use Policy](ACCEPTABLE-USE-POLICY.md).
- **Purchases made with coupon codes explicitly stated as non-refundable.**
- **Service interruptions** caused by factors beyond our control (force majeure) or by excluded events under the [SLA](SLA.md).
- **Third-party payment processing fees**, which are retained by the payment processor (see Section 8.4).

4.1. In exceptional circumstances, we may consider refund requests outside this policy on a case-by-case basis. Contact our billing team with details of your situation; we will always try to act reasonably and in good faith.

## 5. Credits and Add-On Purchases

Credits are the metering unit for AI-powered features (lead discovery, enrichment, scoring, outreach generation, AI assistant). Credit add-on purchases are treated differently from subscriptions:

- **Unused credits:** credit add-on purchases where **no credits have been consumed** may be refunded within **7 days of purchase**.
- **Partially or fully consumed credits:** the purchase becomes **non-refundable** once any credit from the add-on is consumed.
- **Expired credits:** not eligible for refund.
- **Promotional credits:** free or promotional credits have no monetary value and cannot be refunded or exchanged.
- **Credits included in your plan** are part of the subscription fee; unused monthly credits roll over (see [Terms of Service](TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md), Section 7) but are not refundable separately.

Credits have no monetary value and cannot be transferred, sold, or exchanged for cash or other goods.

## 6. Cancellation vs. Refunds

Cancellation and refunds are different things:

- **Cancel at any time.** You may cancel your subscription at any time from your billing settings. Upon cancellation:
  - You retain access to all paid features **until the end of your current billing period** (monthly or annual).
  - Your subscription will not renew, and no further charges apply after the current period ends.
  - Unused monthly and rollover credits are forfeited at the end of the billing period.
  - Your account automatically reverts to the Free plan.
- **Cancellation does not itself trigger a refund** for the current billing period, unless covered by the 14-day money-back guarantee or Section 3 (limited annual cases).
- **Account deletion** (Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Account) cancels the subscription, forfeits unused credits immediately, and initiates data deletion per the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY-POLICY.md).

## 7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

1. **Email us.** Send a refund request to **billing@acquisitionos.com** with the subject line **"Refund Request — [Your Account Email]"**.
2. **Include details.** Provide:
   - Your account email address.
   - The date of purchase and amount charged.
   - The plan and billing period affected (monthly/annual, Pro/Elite).
   - The reason for your request (optional but helpful for improving the Service).
   - The invoice or order ID if available (from your billing history).
3. **Confirmation.** Our billing team will review your request and respond within **3 business days** with a confirmation or follow-up questions.

7.1. Alternatively, you may request a refund through the dashboard at **Settings → Billing → Request Refund** (where available for your plan and purchase).

7.2. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing any refund. Refunds are issued to the **account holder** only.

## 8. Processing Time and Refund Method

Once a refund is approved:

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Review and decision | Within 3 business days of your request |
| Initiation of refund | Within 2 business days of approval |
| **Stripe payments** (cards, wallets) | Refund appears on your statement within **5–10 business days**, depending on your bank |
| **Razorpay payments** (cards, net banking) | Refund appears on your statement within **5–10 business days**, depending on your bank |
| **Razorpay UPI payments** | Typically faster: **1–3 business days** |

8.1. **Refund method.** Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** used for the purchase (via Stripe or Razorpay). We cannot issue refunds to a different card, account, or method — this protects both parties against fraud.

8.2. **Currency.** Refunds are processed in the original currency of the charge (INR for Razorpay/India billing; USD or the original charge currency for Stripe).

8.3. **Notification.** You will receive an email confirmation once the refund has been processed.

8.4. **Processing fees.** Refunds are issued for the amount actually charged. Where a payment processor's fee is non-refundable to us, we may deduct only the portion of the processor fee that is not returned to us; we will itemize any such deduction in the refund confirmation. For the 14-day money-back guarantee, we refund the full subscription fee you paid.

8.5. **Example timeline.** You email a refund request on Monday: we acknowledge and decide by Thursday (3 business days); if approved, we initiate the refund within 2 business days (by the following Monday); the money appears on your card statement 5–10 business days later — typically within two weeks of approval at the latest.

## 9. GST Invoice Adjustments

9.1. For Indian customers, GST is charged at the prevailing rate (currently 18%) and shown on your invoice. When a refund is issued, the corresponding GST must be adjusted.

9.2. **Credit notes.** For each refunded invoice, we issue a **GST credit note** referencing the original invoice, in accordance with GST rules. The credit note reduces your taxable supply for that transaction and reverses the associated GST.

9.3. **Input credit.** If you claimed input tax credit (ITC) on the original invoice using your GSTIN, the credit note allows the corresponding ITC reversal on your side, per applicable GST law. We recommend consulting your tax advisor for treatment on your returns.

9.4. **Timing.** Credit notes are issued within a reasonable period after the refund is processed and are emailed to your registered billing contact.

9.5. For customers outside India, any equivalent local tax adjustment (VAT/sales tax) is handled per local rules, and corrected documents are issued where required.

## 10. Chargeback Policy

10.1. **Contact us first.** If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact **billing@acquisitionos.com** before filing a chargeback with your bank or card issuer. Most billing issues (duplicate charges, failed cancellations, unexpected renewals) can be resolved within a few business days.

10.2. **Our response to chargebacks.** If you file a chargeback without first contacting us:

- Stripe or Razorpay will notify us, and we may **contest the chargeback** with evidence such as billing history, invoice records, and account usage.
- We may **suspend your account and access to the Service** pending resolution of the dispute.
- If the chargeback is resolved in our favor, the original charge stands; if resolved in your favor, your subscription and access end as of the chargeback date.

10.3. **Consequences of abuse.** Repeated or bad-faith chargebacks (for example, filing a chargeback after receiving a refund, or after consuming the Service for the disputed period) may result in termination of your account(s) and refusal of future service.

10.4. A chargeback is **not** a refund request under this policy and does not extend or pause any billing period.

## 11. Exceptions

Refund eligibility may be **denied** in the following circumstances:

- Your request is made after the applicable window (14-day money-back guarantee; 7 days for unused credit add-ons).
- The request concerns a renewal rather than a first purchase.
- Your account was terminated or suspended for violation of the [Terms of Service](TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md) or [Acceptable Use Policy](ACCEPTABLE-USE-POLICY.md) (including spam violations).
- Credits from the add-on in question have been consumed (partially or fully).
- The purchase was made with a coupon explicitly marked non-refundable.
- The claimed issue is an outage or degradation caused by excluded events under the [SLA](SLA.md) (third-party outages, scheduled maintenance, force majeure) — for these, service credits under the SLA are the remedy, not refunds.
- The request concerns third-party payment processing fees, which are retained by the processor.

11.1. Where we deny a refund, we will explain why in writing and, where appropriate, offer alternatives (for example, account credits or a plan change).

## 12. Partial Refunds and Service Credits

In certain situations, partial remedies may apply:

- **Service outages:** if the Service experiences significant downtime (**more than 24 consecutive hours**), affected users may receive a prorated credit to their account. Ongoing service-level failures are governed by the [SLA](SLA.md) service-credit mechanism.
- **Billing errors:** if you were charged incorrectly due to a billing error on our side (for example, a duplicate charge or a charge after cancellation), we will issue a **full refund for the incorrect charge** — this is not subject to the 14-day window.
- **Plan changes:** if you upgrade or downgrade mid-cycle, prorated charges or account credits are applied automatically to keep your billing fair.
- **SLA service credits:** for paid plans that miss the monthly uptime commitment, service credits (a percentage of the monthly fee) are issued per the [SLA](SLA.md) — these are account credits, not cash refunds, and cannot be claimed as a cash refund for the same month.

## 13. Failed Payments and Dunning

13.1. If a payment fails (for example, an expired card or insufficient funds), we retry the charge and notify you by email. Failed attempts do not create a debt or a refund situation — you are only charged for successfully processed periods.

13.2. If the payment failure remains unresolved for **15 days**, your subscription may be suspended and, ultimately, canceled per the [Terms of Service](TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md). You can resume at any time by updating your payment method and re-subscribing.

13.3. If you believe you were charged after a cancellation took effect, contact billing@acquisitionos.com — erroneous charges are fully refunded under Section 12.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced by posting the updated policy with a new "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, by email or in-app notification. The policy in effect at the time of your purchase governs that purchase; changes will not retroactively reduce refund rights you have already accrued.

## 16. Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to give a reason for the 14-day refund?** No. The guarantee applies "no questions asked," though the reason is helpful to us.

**I used the AI features during the 14 days. Can I still get a refund?** Yes — using the Service during the guarantee window does not disqualify you (see Section 2.3).

**Can the refund go to a different card?** No — refunds go only to the original payment method (Section 8.1).

**What happens to my leads and outreach data after a refund?** Your data remains in your account; after the refund your plan reverts to Free. If you delete your account, data is purged within 30 days per the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY-POLICY.md).

**I was billed after I cancelled — is that refundable?** Yes. Charges made after a cancellation took effect are billing errors and are fully refunded (Section 12).
