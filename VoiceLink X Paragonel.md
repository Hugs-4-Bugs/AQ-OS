## **4. What you need to understand before the meeting**

### **VoiceLink is the telephony layer, not the AI brain**

Think of the system as four layers:

**1. Your product — AcquisitionOS**

Prospect, customer, workflow, permissions, CRM and business logic

**2. AI voice agent — e.g. Vapi / LiveKit**

Speech recognition, LLM reasoning, text-to-speech, conversation flow

**3. VoiceLink — telephony connectivity**

Indian phone numbers, SIP, WebSocket, call routing and call events

**4. Indian telecom network**

Actual inbound and outbound calls to phone numbers

The important difference:

- Vapi or LiveKit can power the conversational intelligence.
- VoiceLink can provide the Indian telephony connectivity.
- AcquisitionOS can orchestrate when to call, whom to call, what context to provide, what to record and what happens after the call.

VoiceLink's public product information describes SIP and WebSocket connectivity, REST APIs, routing, call recordings and bot-to-human handoff. Its October 2026 release notes also specifically mention LiveKit and Pipecat SDK integrations for inbound and outbound calling.&#x20;

### **What is SIP?**

SIP stands for *Session Initiation Protocol*. It is a signalling protocol used to establish, manage and terminate voice sessions.

In simple terms: SIP helps connect your voice agent to the phone network.

### **What is WebSocket?**

WebSocket is a persistent, two-way communication connection. For voice AI, it can carry live audio between the telephony provider and your voice-agent service.

It matters because you want natural conversations, not a system that waits for a recording to finish before doing anything.

### **What is a webhook?**

A webhook is an HTTP callback sent by a provider when an event happens.

For example:

1. AcquisitionOS requests an outbound call.
2. VoiceLink starts the call.
3. VoiceLink sends a webhook that the call has started.
4. The customer answers.
5. The call ends.
6. VoiceLink sends a final status event.
7. AcquisitionOS updates the lead and activity history.

Your engineer should validate authentication, retries, duplicate events and event ordering. A webhook that merely works in a demo is not sufficient for a production workflow.

### **What is CPS?**

CPS means *Calls Per Second*. It measures how many new calls a system can initiate per second. It is different from the number of simultaneous calls.

Ask them to explain the relationship between:

- Channels / concurrent calls
- CPS
- Number of DIDs
- Account-level limits
- Per-client allocation in a reseller account

VoiceLink's release notes mention reseller-side channel and CPS allocation.&#x20;

## **8. Compliance, privacy and reliability**

This is not a side topic. It can become a commercial liability if you ignore it.

Ask:

- Which number series are available for the exact use case?
- What KYC documents are needed for Paragonel and its customers?
- Who is responsible for telecom registration and regulatory compliance?
- What consent evidence must be retained before an outbound call?
- How are promotional and transactional calls distinguished?
- What restrictions apply to automated or AI-generated calls?
- How are opt-outs and do-not-call requests enforced?
- Where are call recordings and metadata stored?
- How long are recordings retained?
- Can recordings be disabled?
- Is there a data processing agreement?
- What is the process for security incidents and data deletion?

VoiceLink's website describes 140-series promotional and 1600-series transactional numbers, along with DLT scrubbing for promotional calls. **Q1. What does Paragonel do?**

**Q2. Is AcquisitionOS already live?**

**Q3. Do you have existing customers?**

> We are developing our customer and partnership pipeline and evaluating early use cases. We want to start with an opted-in pilot rather than make unsupported volume commitments. Once the workflow is validated, we can build a realistic rollout forecast.

Do not say you have hundreds of paying customers unless you can prove it.

**Q4. What monthly call volume do you expect?**

> We have not committed to a production volume yet. We would like to understand the pricing at different usage levels and validate the economics through a pilot. After that, we can share a realistic forecast.

**Q5. Why do you want to become a reseller?**

> We want to deliver a complete customer workflow rather than send customers to a separate telephony vendor. A reseller relationship could allow us to package the telephony infrastructure with our software, maintain the customer experience and generate recurring revenue while relying on your telecom infrastructure and support.

**Q6. Why VoiceLink instead of Twilio or another provider?**

> Our priority is reliable Indian telephony, compliance, API access and a commercially sustainable partner model. We are evaluating VoiceLink because of its Indian-number capabilities, SIP/WebSocket support and reseller offering. We will make the decision after validating the integration and commercial terms.

**Q7. What is your expected partnership commitment?**

> Initially, a technical evaluation and a bounded pilot. We would prefer to agree on a repeatable commercial model after validating the customer workflow and operational requirements.

### **Technical questions**

**Q8. Which technology stack do you use?**

> Our backend stack is Java and Spring Boot, with a cloud-hosted application architecture. We are evaluating the voice-agent layer separately from the telephony layer so that we can integrate through documented APIs and supported protocols.

**Q9. Do you need SIP or WebSocket?**

> We want to understand both options. SIP may be suitable for a standard telephony-to-agent connection, while WebSocket may be relevant for real-time audio streaming and custom agent orchestration. We would choose based on the supported architecture and latency requirements.

**Q10. Do you already have a Vapi or LiveKit implementation?**

> We have a preferred architecture in mind, but we are validating the provider-specific integration. We want to review your official documentation, supported SDKs and a working example before finalizing the implementation.

**Q11. Can you handle high call volume?**

> We will design the orchestration and backend for controlled concurrency, retry handling and event processing. We would need your channel, CPS and account-level limits so we can size the integration correctly.

**Q12. How will you handle failed calls and duplicate events?**

> We intend to use idempotent call initiation, unique call identifiers, webhook signature validation, retry handling and a persistent event log. We want to confirm how your API and webhook infrastructure supports these patterns.

### **Commercial questions**

**Q13. Are you ready to purchase a plan today?**

> We are ready to evaluate the plan and agree on the pilot requirements. We would prefer not to make a recurring commitment until the API integration and cost structure have been validated.

**Q14. Can you guarantee volume?**

> Not before the pilot. We would rather provide a realistic forecast based on actual customer demand than promise an artificial number.

**Q15. What margin do you expect?**

> We are looking for a commercially viable reseller structure that leaves room for customer acquisition, onboarding, support and product development. We would like to understand your wholesale pricing tiers before proposing a retail structure.

### **What counts as a successful pilot?**

Don't use vanity metrics such as "we made 100 calls". Use operational outcomes.

| Metric                  | What to measure                                     |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Call initiation success | API requests that successfully create calls         |
| Answer rate             | Calls answered / calls attempted                    |
| Completion rate         | Calls that finish without technical failure         |
| Audio quality           | Dropouts, clipping, interruptions                   |
| Latency                 | End-to-end conversational response delay            |
| Webhook reliability     | Events received, verified and processed             |
| Cost                    | Total telephony + AI cost per completed call        |
| Business result         | Qualified conversations, meetings or completed task |
